Use Case Deep Dives

AI Fleet Optimization

Use Case 1 · Assets & Branch Locations

AI Fleet Optimization —
where things stand today.

Location data — branch hours, addresses, timezone, dock capability — lives in multiple disconnected systems. Timezone and hours errors are not hypothetical: they cause missed customer calls, delivery routing failures, and incorrect information surfaced to customers looking for branch details.

Fleet and equipment data is tracked across four or five separate systems with no master record and no reliable way to know which asset is assigned to which location.

The org hierarchy — Division → Region → Area → Branch — must be consistent for financial rollups and operational reporting. In the current state, that hierarchy can differ across systems, making consolidated reporting an exercise in manual reconciliation before any analysis can begin.

Why start here

The Asset domain has fewer stakeholders than Customer or Product, and the value is immediate and measurable. Clean branch data improves customer-facing accuracy today. Clean fleet data improves delivery routing tomorrow. And because every acquisition brings 1–4 new locations, a repeatable onboarding process here pays dividends 10–14 times a year.

Use Case 1 · Assets & Branch Locations

Business value unlock.

Delivery Operations
Route planning and order tracking require trusted fleet and location data together. Today those live in separate systems; unifying them is a direct delivery optimization win. A routing query that currently requires a dispatcher and three systems becomes a single MDM lookup in milliseconds.
Last-Mile Fulfillment
A branch that knows its own dock capability, service area, and truck compatibility can make accurate delivery promises. Incorrect capability data leads to failed deliveries and customer service escalations. Fleet Compatibility Category links truck type to dock type — a flatbed is never dispatched to a short-dock branch.
M&A Location Onboarding
Each acquisition brings 1–4 new locations needing rapid, clean data setup. A standard onboarding template with validation rules means new locations are live and accurate in hours, not weeks. AI match-and-classify handles the intake; MDM governs the result.
Reporting and Rollup
The org hierarchy — Division → Region → Area → Branch — must be consistent for financial and operational reporting. Clean location master data makes consolidated views automatic, not manual. Cost center mapping is governed and correct from the moment a branch is created.
Use Case 2 · Account & Contact

Customer Personalization —
where things stand today.

SiteOne's customer is an Account — a contractor, landscaper, or commercial buyer — not an individual. The Account is the master node. Contacts hang off accounts. That architecture is sound; the problem is that neither the Account nor the Contact is clean.

Duplicates span UE and Salesforce. A sync assumed to be working was discovered broken via Data Cloud — it took a week to remediate. Contact duplication was deliberately introduced as a workaround to one-contact-per-account system constraints, and the result is the same contractor showing up as three to five separate records across systems.

Marketing cannot target at scale. Loyalty, Tailend, and contractor activation programs require a single trusted account record. Without one, every campaign risks hitting the same customer multiple times with conflicting messages.

Recommended approach

Start with the consolidation pattern: use the mastered record as a source for analytics and marketing only. No write-back to source systems until data trust is established. Governance and definitions before system work. The risk of merging customers incorrectly — disrupting AR, credit lines, and sales history — is real, and the right answer is to build confidence before changing operational systems.

Use Case 2 · Account & Contact

Business value unlock.

Personalization at Scale
One account, one message, across branch and digital channels. Customer data virtualized in Data Cloud for segmentation and activation. Courtney's team can run campaigns with confidence — knowing the contractor they're targeting is one record, not five.
Cross-Sell and Up-Sell Revenue
Trusted account records linked to purchase history unlock product recommendations. Which contractors buy mulch but not edging? Which accounts haven't purchased in 60 days? These queries only work when the account record is clean and unduplicated.
Customer Retention Insight
Understanding why accounts leave requires understanding when they were last active, what they bought, and which branch served them. None of this is possible without a unified customer record. Fix the account, and the retention data that already exists becomes usable.
M&A Customer Integration
AI match-and-classify ingests acquired customer records, identifies overlap with existing SiteOne accounts, and preserves five-year sales history and AR. External match capability can profile overlap before close — so SiteOne knows what it's buying before the ink is dry.
Use Case 3 · Supplier & Supplier Location

Supplier Mastering —
where things stand today.

Up to eight distinct supplier records exist for a single vendor across US and Canadian systems. The true supplier count is unknown. There is no reliable way to know total spend per supplier, which contracts are in effect, or which locations a supplier serves.

The supplier onboarding form is mostly automated submission with limited credit checks. Branch-level staff can enter a new supplier on the fly with no validation — and they do. Each of SiteOne's 10 to 14 annual acquisitions brings an unresolved overlap problem: which of the acquired company's suppliers are already in the SiteOne network? Today, answering that question is a manual project.

The private brand connection

SiteOne cannot expand its private label program without knowing which supplier produces which product. Clean supplier-to-product linkage is the prerequisite for private brand expansion. That linkage does not exist today.

Use Case 3 · Supplier & Supplier Location

Business value unlock.

Procurement Optimization
A trusted supplier record enables contract compliance checks, category-level benchmarking, and multi-location negotiation leverage. SiteOne can see total spend per supplier for the first time — and use that visibility to negotiate better terms across the enterprise.
M&A Pre-Close Analysis
Informatica's external match capability profiles acquired-company supplier overlap before integration closes. SiteOne knows which contracts are duplicated, which are complementary, and which to consolidate — before the acquisition is complete. This alone justifies the investment at 10–14 deals per year.
Search Before Create
Implementing search-before-create in Salesforce and branch onboarding apps stops duplicate supplier records at the point of entry. Reference 360 standardizes tax ID, legal name, and key attributes. Data quality improves from the first day of go-live.
Private Brand Enablement
Clean supplier-to-product linkage is the foundation for private label expansion. Identify which suppliers have capacity, which products to migrate to private label, and where margin improvement is greatest — using data that already exists in the system, properly connected.
Use Case 4 · Base Product, Variant, Category, Pack Config

Product & Packaging —
where things stand today.

PIMcore requires costly maintenance and custom code for what should be standard data quality and attribution. It cannot support multiple simultaneous product hierarchies — finance, sales territory, hazard classification — without duplicating the product record.

Packaging information is not held against the product record. Pallet weight, case dimensions, and unit-of-measure relationships are unknown or inconsistent. This is not a minor gap: it means routing AI cannot calculate optimal truck load, Blue Yonder PO matching fails, and the 20% of products that Athena cannot automatically attribute have no governed review workflow.

The base-variant model is inconsistent. Variants — size, grade, species — are not reliably linked to their base products, breaking inventory accuracy, planogram management, and cross-sell logic at the source.

The tech stack opportunity

Eliminating PIMcore and the duplicate MySQL instance reduces maintenance cost, removes a manual export/import cycle, and gives product teams a single governed workspace in P360 for all enrichment work. The product domain is also the one where AI attribution is most advanced — the foundation is there, it needs to be completed and governed.

Use Case 4 · Base Product, Variant, Category, Pack Config

Business value unlock.

Packaging-Driven Route Optimization
Pack configuration — dimensions and weight — tied to the product record enables routing AI to calculate the optimal truck load per delivery. Match the right product to the right vehicle type and dock configuration. This connection between Product and Asset is one of the highest-value cross-domain relationships in the entire program.
Blue Yonder PO and Invoice Matching
GS1-certified packaging hierarchy unlocks automated PO-to-invoice matching in Blue Yonder. AI can back-fill packaging levels from historical PO data where they are currently unknown — the answer is already in the data, it just needs to be extracted and governed.
Simplified Technology Stack
Eliminating PIMcore and the duplicate MySQL instance reduces maintenance cost, removes a manual export/import cycle, and gives product teams a single governed workspace in P360. Repeatable, syndicated exports replace one-off scripts. Time to market for new products improves.
Multiple Hierarchies from One Model
P360 supports simultaneous product hierarchies — finance, sales territory, hazard class, and procurement category can each have their own view without duplicating the product record. Different teams get the view they need; the data is maintained once.
