☠️ What Contaminates Your Data

Bad Data Spreads
Like Contamination.

In food safety, contamination moves fast and quietly — invisible until people get sick. Bad master data does the same thing. By the time it surfaces in a wrong report, a failed recall, or a missed supplier risk, it's already been in your systems for years.

🦠

The Duplicate Pathogen

Three records for the same supplier breed like bacteria. Each one spawns inconsistent SKU mappings, wrong order histories, and a recall you can't trace to the source.

📦

The Missing Lineage Toxin

Cleaning data without preserving lineage is like washing the kitchen but throwing away the HACCP log. The auditor wants the history. "We deleted it" is not an answer.

🏢

The Hidden Hierarchy Mold

You buy romaine from three "different" suppliers — until a recall reveals they all grow in the same valley under the same parent company. One bad harvest. Total exposure.

🕰️

The Stale Data Spoilage

A golden record that isn't connected to live supply chain signals is produce past its sell-by date. Looks fine in the system. Already a liability on the shelf.
