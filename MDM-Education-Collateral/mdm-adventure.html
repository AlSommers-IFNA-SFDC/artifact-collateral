💥 The Canonical Anchor

Fire the Cannon.
Claim the Truth.

Every great pirate ship has a cannon — and in this adventure, ours fires Canonical records. The C·A stamped on the barrel stands for Canonical Anchor: the single, authoritative shot that ends the debate between conflicting silos.

Unlike a simple "golden record" (a dusty treasure map copy left in a drawer), the Canonical Anchor is loaded and live. It fires the best-verified truth for every field — name, address, revenue, hierarchy — across every system simultaneously.

One shot. One truth. All silos stand down.
